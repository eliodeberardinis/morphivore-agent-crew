--- gdd-scoped §2.9 ---
2.9 The Bestiary

The Bestiary is where the meta lives, a single screen: **rows are limb count (1–6), columns are family (5).** A cell is a family at a rank that opens into that family's **intensity ladder** (Pale → Dusk → Deep → Clash → Rage), so 30 cells hold **150 forms** — three axes rendered as two. Reached forms show in full colour with their name; unreached ones as a **dark silhouette.** It also keeps every **emblem** any bloodline ever took, permanently — **the line ends, the record does not.** Opened from the pause and main menus (discovery survives death); the header shows **discovered / total** and the next milestone.
