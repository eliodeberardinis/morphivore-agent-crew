# Morphivore — How to Play

**▶ Play in your browser:** https://eliodeb87.itch.io/morphivore

No download, no account, no install. It runs in the page.

---

## What you are

A one-limb white cube with a mouth and no plan, dropped on an island where everything is
either food or something that thinks *you* are.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| **WASD** or **arrow keys** | move |
| **Left-click** | pounce |
| **Hold right-click** | lock on to the nearest creature in front of you |
| **Space** | dash |
| **Q** | spit out your oldest colour |

A gamepad works too: left stick to move, **A** pounce, **LB** lock on, **RT** dash, **B**
spit.

## The one thing to understand

**You are what you eat.** Every coloured creature you swallow puts its colour into one of
your limbs, and the colours you are carrying decide what you become:

| Colour | You become | The trade |
|---|---|---|
| **Yellow** | Brawler | Long health bar, heavy damage, turns like a boulder |
| **Red** | Leaper | Fastest thing alive, dies to two mistakes |
| **Blue** | Sniper | Huge reach — you hit things before they reach you |
| **Purple** | Stalker | Quick and long-armed, middling everywhere else |
| **Grey** | Apex | Strong all round, and rare |

There is no levelling underneath this. **Your body is your build**, and your next meal can
change it. Watch the top-left of the screen — it names what you currently are.

## How to eat something

1. **Pounce it** (left-click) until it falls over. A standing creature bounces you off.
2. **Pounce the fallen body** to eat it. Its colour becomes yours.

Creatures fight back the same way: they stop, crouch, telegraph, then launch at you. You can
dodge it by moving once they commit — an orange halo warns you when something has you
locked.

## Healing

**Only white grazers heal you.** They are smaller than everything else and carry a floating
red heart, so you can spot one across a field. They carry no colour, so eating one heals but
never changes you.

**Hunting does not heal.** That is the trade — meat changes you, grazers keep you alive.

## Winning

Each biome has an **Alpha**, and it does not arrive on a timer. It wakes once you have eaten
enough of that biome, in enough different shapes, to be worth its attention. The HUD shows
both counters:

```
WAKE THE ALPHA:  FORMS 2/3  ·  PREY EATEN 11/15
```

When it wakes, the screen flashes red and it comes for you. It is the champion of whichever
colour you ate the most of, at your own size, and **you cannot outrun it** — you can dash to
buy space, but not to escape.

Beat it and eat it: you take its emblem, grow a limb, and move to the next territory.
**Five biomes, five Alphas.** Survive all five and you win.

If your health reaches zero, the run ends. There are no lives — start again as a new
one-limb cube.

## Tips

- **Eat before you fight.** Top up on grazers while the Alpha counters are still filling.
- **Variety matters as much as volume.** The forms counter only moves when you become
  something you have not been in this biome yet, so vary what you eat.
- **Spit deliberately.** **Q** drops your oldest colour and frees that limb — useful when a
  colour you are stuck with is the wrong tool for what is in front of you.
- **A dodged pounce leaves an enemy exposed.** Move as it commits, then hit it while it
  recovers.
