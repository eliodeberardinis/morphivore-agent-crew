PRE-BUILD DECLARATION — Assignment #6
Morphivore · Elio De Berardinis

1. Generated manually, inconsistently, or not at all?

Emblems. emblems.json is the one file in my GDD's §3.3 content contract never
written — not by hand, not by my #3/#4 crews. Twenty-five: one per Alpha, five
biomes × five family champions.

2. The rule every piece must satisfy?

§2.5: an emblem is "a trophy off a defeated rival, granting no power of its own
(powers come from panels)." The §3.3 contract row ends "(no powers)." An emblem may
only open the next biome and enable breeding.

3. What failure looks like?

An emblem granting an ability or stat — "+15% dash", "aura of dread." It breaks
§2.8 — panels are the run's only power source — and compounds: emblems are permanent
and bred true, while panels are shed at capacity. My revision log records removing
exactly this. A generator reaches for it anyway, because everywhere else "emblem"
means relic.
