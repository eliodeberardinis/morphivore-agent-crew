# Requirement: Generator invariants and seed refusal  (GDD 3.3)

**What the design requires**
At generation time the generator must guarantee five invariants and refuse the seed otherwise: (1) every trait gate in a biome has a reachable carrier outside the terrain that trait unlocks; (2) the rolled palette contains enough distinct families to keep the reachable pool at least ~1.5× the form gate (min_distinct_colours = 4 in Prairies/Wetlands/Mountains, 5 in Beach and Volcanic); (3) standing population plus respawn can supply the biome's coloured-prey count with margin; (4) the plausible most-eaten colour is present in that biome's Alpha roster; (5) a trait required by a later biome remains obtainable given the expected trait set on entry.

**How you could tell it works in-game**
No generated biome can be entered in a state where a required trait carrier is behind its own gate, where the form gate is unclearable, or where prey run out; failing rolls are rejected and re-rolled rather than shipped.

**Data it implies**
biomes.json: min_distinct_colours, population/respawn, Alpha roster, gate thresholds

**Worked example from the document**
A 3-family Volcanic roll would clear the 11-form gate (3 × 5 = 15) but collapse slack from 2.3× to 1.36×, so it is refused.

--- gdd-scoped §2.7 ---
2.7 Biomes — escalating traversal complexity

Each biome is procedurally generated and holds one **Alpha lair.** The five run from the ecosystem's soft margins to its heart: 1. **Prairies** — open grassland, **no gates;** where you learn the loop. 2. **Wetlands** — ponds and channels; **Fins** reach roughly half the map. 3. **Mountains** — **Claws** for the peaks *and* a **Coat** for the cold: the first two-trait biome, the difficulty step-up. 4. **Beach** — tidal flats and cliffs; combines **Fins** and **Claws,** and introduces the last trait, **Heat,** at the volcanic vents. 5. **Volcanic** — the finale; lava, sheer rock and flooded caves demand **Fins, Claws, Coat** and **Heat** at once. The trait-introduction count is **0 → 1 → 2 → 1 → 0.** **Coat carriers live in the Mountains foothills,** below lethal cold, so the trait that survives the peaks is obtainable without first surviving them (a generator invariant, §3.3). **Common food everywhere, strength out in the perils.** Ordinary creatures wander so you can always find a meal, but the mix darkens with distance from your entrance, and **the Alpha lair sits at the strong end.** Top-tier meat clusters in **defended pockets** (Clash never wanders) that **bite back** with swarming packs and elites, turning the form gate into an **exploration** gate. And **each biome's palette is rolled per run,** so which forms you reach, which colour you eat most, and which Alpha comes all cascade from that roll.
