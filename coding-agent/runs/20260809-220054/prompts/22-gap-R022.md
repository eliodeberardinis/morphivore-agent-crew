# Requirement: Instant mutation with wound preserved  (GDD 2.3, 2.4b)

**What the design requires**
The player mutates the instant the resolved form changes; the Health bar is re-cut to the new form's maximum and the player's wound fraction is preserved rather than its absolute number — mutation never heals nor costs Health.

**How you could tell it works in-game**
Eating a meal that changes the resolution immediately swaps the body and stats; if the player was at 50% Health before, they are at 50% of the new bar afterward, whether that bar grew or shrank.

--- gdd-scoped §2.3 ---
2.3 Health — the bar you chose, and how you refill it

You have **one** meter: **Health**; at zero you die and the run ends permanently. Health is a property of your current **form:** limbs set how much animal you are, colour how it is spent. Mutating re-cuts the bar and **preserves your wound, not your number** (you keep the same *fraction*); it never heals or costs a point. **Grazers refill it.** Harmless, **no-colour, limbless white** herbivores scattered thinly across every biome; they never fight and bolt the instant you lock them. Eat one to recover a chunk of **current** Health — a grazer heals but cannot change you (nothing to take). Grazer flee speed is pinned in `creatures.json` **below every form's dash speed** (Speed × family Dash multiplier), so no form is ever locked out of healing. **Hunting does not heal you.** Coloured prey drives mutation, Alpha progress and litter width, nothing for your wounds; the two meals even look different (prey **streams into a limb,** a grazer **dissolves into the body**). **Grazers don't count toward the Alpha's eat-count.**
