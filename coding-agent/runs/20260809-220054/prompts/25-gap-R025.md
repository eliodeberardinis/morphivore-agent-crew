# Requirement: Procedural body rendering of forms  (GDD 2.4b, 2.6a)

**What the design requires**
One procedural system renders all 150 bodies from the cube: the authored socket layout gives the silhouette (reading family), the number of limb-segments reads rank, and saturation reads tier; traits render on the cube's main face so limbs stay free for colour.

**How you could tell it works in-game**
Two forms of the same family at different ranks share a silhouette but differ in segment count; the same form at Pale vs Rage differs in saturation; a trait appears on the cube face, never on a limb.
